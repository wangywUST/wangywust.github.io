2. Discrete channel impulse response h[l]

Each slot is one tap. Bar height is |h[l]|. When several paths enter the same slot, their complex gains are added, so phase can reinforce or cancel the tap.

3. Tapped-delay-line interpretation

The receiver forms delayed signal copies x[n−l], multiplies each one by h[l], and sums all branches.

Key conclusion

Modeling note: a physical path has a continuous delay, but a discrete-time channel stores coefficients only on the sampling grid. A single tap may therefore contain one path or the complex sum of several paths whose delays are not separately resolvable.
